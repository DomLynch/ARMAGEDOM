## Pipeline
- OpenTrident Dev, 28 Oct 2026: MPFB2 2.0.18 in Blender 5.2 (tool only, none of its code ships), hand-authored rows, 106-joint Mixamo skeleton factory, reduced to 13.5k triangles with 1024 px WebP textures and meshopt. Per body: 2 draws, 106 joints, no `Layer_`/`Sword`/`Weapon` nodes, no weapon. Build report `report.md`, packs `PACKS.md`, per-asset record `assets.json` (this folder, copied unchanged).
- Idle: `build.mjs` (from the unmerged `origin/art/hub-npcs`, one change: grade `none`, since these bodies are already coloured) drops every clip and retargets ONE clip from the cleared Mixamo set (the clip library at `/srv/dev-jobs/opentrident-site/models/mixamo/clips/<id>.glb`) onto the body by bone direction (hips world delta, other bones aimed at the clip's posed bone vectors, clip roll kept, fingers and toes at rest), resamples, prunes, re-applies meshopt. Run on the VPS: `node art/hub-npcs/build.mjs <body.glb> <clip.glb> <dst.glb> none`.
- Rows are generated by `register.mjs` (same measure and row shape as `art/makehuman-crowd/register.mjs`; refuses to run twice). `scale` 1 for all three (heights 1.68 to 1.80 m are inside 1.5 to 1.95 m). Every clip role in the row points at `Idle` (the file has one clip). Rig `mixamo-106`, motion `donor-knife`.

## Why these clips (candidates tried and judged by looking at them)
- Marsh: `107900901-breathing-idle`, as the first pass suggested. Upright, arms loose, slow breath, hips sway about 10 cm.
- Okafor: wanted a clipboard-style hold. Tried `119260901-looking-through-files-low` (bends over, head 1 m), `101780901-writing` (a seated desk pose), `110210901-looking` (ducks and waves), `118030901-thinking` (hand gestures), `119280901-searching-files-high`; picked `118910901-standing-w-briefcase-idle`: upright, calm, one hand carrying something low, head level. The briefcase or clipboard itself is not modelled.
- Reyes: wanted hand to ear. Mixamo has no radio or earpiece clip in this set; the nearest is a phone call. Tried `119140901-talking-on-a-cell-phone` (more wandering gesture), `119340901-texting-while-standing` (upright and very still, hands low, head down at a phone, but no ear), `101330904-listening-to-music` (odd cupped hands) and `115170901-neutral-idle` (retarget broke: feet 4 m below the floor, dropped). Picked `119170901-talking-on-phone`: one open hand at the ear (closest hand to the head joint 0.17 m), other on the hip. Honest cost: it is a 39 s loop that includes one step of about 40 cm forward and back (hips travel 0.42 m, left foot 0.22 m); the other two stay within about 13 cm. No handset is modelled, so the raised hand reads as hand to ear or head, not a radio.

## Idle playback (what OpenTrident did not verify)
- `Web/tests/hub-npcs.test.js` plays each NPC's `Idle` through the engine's own `DonorMotion` at 11 phases and asserts: exactly one clip, every role points at it; head above hips above both feet by more than 0.3 m; feet on the ground (ankle y between -0.05 and 0.25 m); finite matrices; head and hands move at least 1 cm; the spine stays within 0.5 m of its start (measured about 0.13, 0.12 and 0.44 m); the loop closes (end within 25 cm of start). It also checks bytes, sha256, `bodySha256` against OpenTrident's record, the one `npc/` folder, 106 joints, at most 16 draws, no weapon nodes, size under 600 KB.
- `shoot.mjs` + `idle.html` repeat the check in headless Chromium (Apple GPU, muted, 21 phases) on the game's own central-hub camera rig, lights and no tone mapping, and take the stills. All three: Idle plays, upright, pose changes (hand travel 0.21, 0.14 and 1.15 m), loop seam 0.00 to 0.01 m. The lowest skinned vertex sits 0.07 to 0.11 m below the model origin, so the stills lift each pose to put it on the floor.
- **The engine does not animate a standing human's idle.** `DonorMotion.update()` for a stationary entity samples `Idle` at phase 0 on every tick (the test asserts this). The NPCs breathe only if the Hub wiring PR drives `sample(idle, phase)` itself, the way `engine/remote-survivors.js` offsets its idle clock. Crowd residents behave the same today.
