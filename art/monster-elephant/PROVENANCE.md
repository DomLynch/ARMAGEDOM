# Elephant (original-elephant), 28 Oct 2026

A realistic, scarred, ash-grey elephant: healed slash scars, a few old dried wounds, tumour-like growths, one long tusk snapped at the tip and a short stub, ragged torn ears, small dark eyes. Nothing glows, no spikes or horns (Dom's ruling, 28 Oct: 2030 realistic game). Model scale 5, 2.5 x the dog rig (the wolf is 2.5, the dog 2).

Pipeline, all CPU on the VPS (`/srv/dev-jobs/elephant/`, no GPU, no paid service): `run.sh` runs `elephant_mesh.py` (Blender 5.2 headless), `elephant_tex.py` (numpy + Pillow), `elephant_pack.py`, `elephant_swap.mjs` (gltf-transform core) and `gltf-transform meshopt --level medium`.
- Rig reused unchanged: the wolf's `QuadRig`, 31 joints, inverse bind matrices, node names (`wolf`, `QuadRig`, `WeaponDrawn`) and all seven clips (Bite, Death, Flee, Hurt, Idle, Run, Walk) are the wolf GLB's own, kept byte-identical by editing the GLB, not re-exporting from Blender (clip durations and fps untouched). Source `Web/public/assets/wolf/wolf.glb`, sha256 `16e0ed917e080afe971315c1c14c58e68b8e11cdb385924ba726420cc8036030`.
- Mesh: swept tubes and ellipsoids built in the wolf's rest pose (body, head with twin domes, trunk, two tusks, two torn ears, four thick legs with pads and nails, tail with tuft, three growths, eyes, lip). Every vertex is weighted to the nearest existing joints by position (4 influences max); trunk, tusks and eyes follow the head joint, ears their ear joints, the lip the jaw joint. No new bones.
- Size: about 6.2k triangles, GLB 307,504 bytes (budget 8k and 600 KB), sha256 `e1400fa1857687d1f4e7d920887a89d469686e34fec81a60540048586ad9aa50`. Colour atlas 512 WebP; the emissive atlas is all black (nothing glows). Painted per texel from the rest-pose position and body part, so seams never show.
- Contact: the bite vertex is a centre-line trunk vertex (index 4106, found by `elephant_measure.mjs`); in the Bite active window it sits about 0.597 rig metres ahead (x 0) and 0.18 high, so reach 4.95 = 0.597 x 5 x 1.265 x 1.3225 and mouthOffset 0, at about 1.5 world units, inside the player body band. The same script reproduces the wolf's own numbers (vertex 3145: 2.37, 0.25) as a check.
- Game rows: `elephant_attack` move (damage 16), rig row `original-elephant`, 150 HP, radius 1.3, speed 2.2; the Elephant and Castle creature slot (replaces the dog / roach there, kept under `off`); switched off with `?noelephant=1` or `enabled: false`.
- Look changes after Dom's realism ruling: removed the glowing red eyes, glowing wounds and ember cracks and the glowing lumps (emissive map is empty), removed the eight dorsal bone spurs, smaller dark eyes, scale 6 to 5, radius, reach and aim heights re-measured.
- Stills: `stills/elephant-bite.png` (375 x 812 in game, Mac Chromium headless, fake clock, `elephant-still.mjs`), `stills/elephant-model-3q.png` and `elephant-model-front.png` (Blender Cycles model views).
