# Spider: a large real spider, hairy, eight legs, muted browns (28 Oct 2026)

Look ruling (Dom, 28 Oct, supersedes the brief's look notes): a 2030 realistic game, grounded and believable, no glowing eyes, no fantasy flourish. A first build had cream chevron markings and glossy eyes; it was replaced by this one: huntsman/tarantula-like, matte muted browns, faint mottling, dull dark eyes, and shrunk to a believable size (about 1.3 m across the legs in game, a big dog at most).

Strategy ruled GO on six new monsters (rat king, eel, crow, spider, centipede, elephant); this is the spider. One PR, tier-b.

Pipeline, all CPU (VPS `/srv/dev-jobs/spider/`, no GPU, no paid service): `run.sh` runs `atlas.py` (Pillow/numpy), `build.py` (Blender 5.2 headless), `gltf-transform meshopt --level medium`, then `anim_cmp.py`. `find_fangs.mjs` reads the two bite vertices out of the final GLB; `viewer.html` / `viewer.mjs` take the model stills.

Rig kept: the roach rig, `ARM_RoachRig`, all 27 joints with their names and bind pose, six clips (`roach_bite/death/hit/idle/run/walk`). No new bone, no new clip. Source GLB: the original roach, sha256 `d38016af...8922` (the same file `art/roach-v3/` starts from).
- Mesh: a new single skinned mesh `ARM Giant Spider` (glTF node `ARM_Giant_Spider`), one material `Dark hairy chitin`. Rounded abdomen, cephalothorax with eight dull dark eyes (two large, six small), two fangs on the mandible bones, two pedipalps on the head and antenna bones, two spinnerets, about 330 thin hair cones with a dark root and a tan tip.
- Eight legs on the roach's six: four pairs riding the three roach leg chains. Front pair on `leg_0/shin_0/foot_0`, the second and third pairs on `leg_1` (the two added legs), the rear pair on `leg_2`. Leg vertices are weighted by distance from the body (`leg_n`, then `shin_n`, then `foot_n`); body vertices copy the skin weights of the nearest original vertex. The mesh is built 0.72 m across the legs and then shrunk by 0.62 about the origin (`SIZE` in `build.py`; the bones are untouched), so it is 0.45 m across at model scale 1 and about 1.3 m at the manifest scale 3, the roach's scale and `roach_bite_scaled` attack.
- Clip timing: the source clips are 30 fps; `build.py` sets the scene to 30 fps (the factory 24 fps scene trims the last frame off every clip). Durations equal the source: bite 0.6, death 0.8, hit 0.3, idle 1.6, run 0.4, walk 0.6 s (`anim_cmp.py`: joints equal, 27 of 27).
- Size: 5,144 triangles (Blender count), 3,888 vertices in the GLB, GLB 292,740 bytes (roach 525,280); albedo 1024 WebP, ORM 512, normal 512. Phone budget (under 600 KB, under 8k triangles) met.
- Textures (`atlas.py`, painted from rules, no reference image and no third-party asset): abdomen top in muted dark brown with faint lighter mottling; cephalothorax with a faint median band and streaks; legs with faint tan bands at the knee and above the foot; hair strip dark to muted tan; dark brown fang gradient; eye texel (dull dark brown, a faint sheen, no glint). Roughness (ORM green) is matte throughout (0.45 to 0.9, eyes 0.4). Normal map is a Sobel of the luminance.
- Bite contact: `contact.canines` are the two fang-tip vertices (1674, 1777 of `ARM_Giant_Spider`), measured in the final GLB with the skinned position; `calfBand` and `calfFaces` are the roach's (they describe the player's calf, not the creature).
- Hashes: GLB sha256 `da3358f6c420bc570fb8e852c1d2adbc6f36076ff64ef31c8453151f5d2b9355` (pinned in `manifest-hollow.json`, model `original-spider`).

Registration (data rows, no new engine code beyond the off switch): manifest model `original-spider`, creature definition and recipe `spider-crypt`, rig capabilities row (`species` spider, `optionalMonster` true, `enabled` true, `offParam` `nospider`, the same mechanism as the crow), catalogue support and reach, behaviour (baseline) and intent (ambush), the low-melee rig lists, target heights, finisher body row. No engine code changed.

Placement: area7 (the station; dark, ruined, interior-feeling), the existing creature slot `area7-station-rat-v1`, cycle B only (it held a dog). Cycle A is the live cycle on the server and keeps the rat, so `server/data/spawns.json` is untouched; a payout seed migration (`20261028000022`, with its down file, not applied) adds the creature rate for the new kind. Off switch: `?nospider=1`, or set `enabled` to false in `creatures.json` (`rigCapabilities.original-spider`); the slot then uses its cycle A creature, the rat.

Not checked: a real phone (loading time, frame pacing, feel), the live server kill cash for the spider (cycle B kills are refused by the writer today, as for the dog), audio (the spider uses the roach's attack sounds).
