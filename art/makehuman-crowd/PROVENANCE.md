# MakeHuman crowd bodies (28 Oct 2026)

30 realistic, non-supernatural MakeHuman V2 bodies reduced by OpenTrident Dev, registered as deferred appearance rows in `Web/public/assets/manifest-hollow.json` (`models` and `files`, ids `mh-<id>`, files `Web/public/assets/crowd/mh-<id>.glb`, 16.1 MB raw, 11.8 MB gz for all 30).

- Source and reduction: VPS `/root/mh-reduce/out/<id>.glb`, report `reduction-report.md` (13.5k triangles each, 1024 px WebP textures, 106 Mixamo joints, 12 clips, no `Layer_`/`Sword`/`Weapon` nodes). Upstream: `/srv/dev-jobs/opentrident-site/models/makehuman/<id>.glb` and `models/makehuman-gen/body-<nnnn>.glb`.
- Rows are generated by `register.mjs` (measures bytes, sha256, bind height and hips height at the first Idle frame; refuses to run twice). Hashes are pinned in both `models` and `files`, which `scripts/verify-assets.mjs` checks at build.
- Rig: `mixamo-106`, the same 106-joint Mixamo skeleton as the marked hunter (`pitborn-106`), so a ragdoll row for the 106 body can serve these bodies later.
- Clips: the 9 Mixamo clips the game uses (Idle, Walk, Run, Attack, Guard, Roll, Hit, Death, Heavy). Roles Mixamo did not supply (`return`, `riposte`, `skill_pommel`, `parry`, `block`, `deflected`) point at the nearest clip in the row's `clips`. The bodies carry no weapon, so a crowd resident's knife moves are bare-handed swings.
- Hips rotation: tested and not an issue in this build. The Hips rotation track is authored against the body's own Hips rest rotation (about 100 degrees about X, the same frame the Pitborn hunter's clips use), so all 9 clips play upright through `DonorMotion` and in the game (stills below, `Web/tests/crowd-bodies.test.js` asserts head above hips above feet for idle, walk, run, attack, heavy, guard and hit on every active body, and a grounded head at the end of Death). The flip OpenTrident saw comes from the three `Stress_*` clips (Roll90, ForwardRoll, OverheadRoll), which rotate the root on purpose and are never mapped to a role, and from retargeting clips onto a different rest rotation. If a body ever needs it, drop the track with a manifest-side change; none does today.
- Licence: Mixamo animation clips cleared by Dom 2026-10-23. The MakeHuman body, hair and clothing licence statement per asset is owed by OpenTrident Dev; it is not in the repo and not verified here.

Stills (`stills/`, headless Chromium on the Apple GPU, muted, 375 wide at 3x): `east-idle`, `east-walk`, `east-death-d15`, `east-death-d30` (East, two residents wearing `mh-gen-v2-0048`) and `active-set-clips` (the 8 active bodies in Idle, Walk, Run and Death at two phases each).
