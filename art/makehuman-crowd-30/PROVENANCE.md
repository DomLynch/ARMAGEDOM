# Crowd batch 2: 30 bodies in worn 2030 clothing (28 Oct 2026)

30 realistic adult MakeHuman bodies (`mh-worn-01` .. `mh-worn-30`), 15 men and 15 women, registered as deferred appearance rows (`models` + `files` in `Web/public/assets/manifest-hollow.json`, files `Web/public/assets/crowd/mh-worn-NN.glb`) and as pool rows in `Web/src/data/crowdBodies.json` with `active: false`, so nothing changes in the game until Dom or Lead flips rows on. Ragdoll rows (`physicsRigs.json`) are measured per body and not live. Batch 1 (`art/makehuman-crowd/`) is untouched.

- **No repeats.** `diff-vs-first-30.md` lists the first 30 (19 shipped, 11 withheld) against the new 30 by sex, skin, hair and suit: 0 repeats of a first-30 (skin, hair, suit) triple, 0 repeats inside batch 2, and no new id collides with a batch 1 id or one of the 19 shipped.
- **Worn 2030 clothing.** The CC0 pack has stock suits only. Each body wears one of 9 suits recoloured from a faded, dark, dust and rust set (shirt and trousers apart, scuffed shoes, natural or grey hair, no neon), with dirt mottling and sun fade. No armour, no hat, no mask, no weapon, no creature head.
- **Logo prints.** The stock shirts carry a round MakeHuman logo. The new palette mode `worn` (`tools-patch/worn_mode.py`) fills those prints with plain cloth, and the 375 px stills show none. Trouser rectangles were placed on a UV grid (`suit_sheet.py`), and 16 bodies were rebuilt after a first pass showed a wrong-coloured leg.
- **Build.** VPS (Hugging Face CPU cannot run it: it needs Blender with MPFB2, the CC0 packs and the Mixamo clip library, none of which are in a public repo). `crowd30_rows.py` writes the rows (seed 20301028, ids `body-0101..`; 34 built, 30 kept, 4 afro-hair spares dropped for a noisy hair texture), `build_batch.sh` runs the OpenTrident MakeHuman factory without a gear set, then `reduce2.mjs` (13.5k triangles, 1024 px WebP, meshopt). The factory copy used is private to this job, with `tools-patch/vrm_variants.py` (a 3-line hook for the `worn` mode) on top; the shared factory folder was not touched.
- **Numbers** (`reduction-report.md`): 13.5k triangles, 2 skinned draws (cap 16), 106 joints, 12 clips, 434 to 621 KB per GLB (10.5 MB gz for all 30; they are deferred and downloaded per area, never preloaded), glTF validator 0 errors on all 34 (the meshopt extension warning is expected).
- **Licence.** Same statement and packs as batch 1 (`makehuman_system_assets_cc0`, `skins01_cc0` only), CC0; per-row text in the manifest. Not independently re-verified per asset.
- **Stills** (`stills/`, headless Chromium, software GL, 375 css px wide at 3x): `sheets-full` (all 30, idle), `sheets-torso` (all 30, chest close-ups for the print check), `samples` (5 bodies, Walk, one per image).
- **Not checked:** a physical phone, frame pacing, and the in-game crowd draw (rows are inactive). Hair on a few curly-hair bodies reads slightly noisy; a neckline trim patch on some shirts reads as a tear.
