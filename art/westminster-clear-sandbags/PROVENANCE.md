# Westminster: stairside sandbag stack painted out

Dom's phone report (live 304): the player and the chained Pitborn hunter stuck on the sandbags at the foot of the lion plinth stairs. The sandbags were backdrop paint with one blocker ("Stairside sandbags", no depth mask), not a 3D prop.

Same method as `art/south-clear-crates-hedgehog` (#421): `clear_road.py` (a copy of that script, CPU: numpy, OpenCV, Pillow) fills a hand-drawn mask (`mask-westminster.json`, 19 points around the stack, full-image pixels) with the colour and light of the surrounding ground plus texture quilted from clean source rectangles (`src-westminster.json`: paving right of the lamp, the stair treads left of the stack, the plinth-foot paving above it), a smooth border offset and a 16 px feathered fade.

- Env: `CLEAR_QUALITY=92 CLEAR_DILATE=15 CLEAR_FEATHER=16 CLEAR_SEED=11 CLEAR_BAND=200 CLEAR_GAIN=1.5 CLEAR_PALE=105 CLEAR_PALE_SHARE=0.02 CLEAR_GUARD=15`.
