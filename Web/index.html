Controls

Phone: portrait or landscape. Left stick moves; push beyond the rim to sprint using stamina. Tap an action in your facing direction. Drag on an action to aim following strikes; drag Guard to turn your defence. Hold Guard to block, press just before eligible melee hits to parry.

Desktop: WASD move · mouse aims · LMB Slash · RMB Stab · Q Heavy · E Special · Space Dodge · hold F Guard · hold Shift Sprint · C Next weapon · R Retry.

Attacks, dodge and sprint spend stamina. Recover when not attacking or sprinting; recovery is slower while guarding. Low stamina dims actions; exhausted fighters must recover before attacking or dodging.

Pistol: collect/equip with the equipment button or G. Left stick turns and aims the whole body; hold Fire to keep firing. Assist fully corrects small pistol aim errors. Flame locks a nearby visible target while firing; drag Flame to aim or turn away. Flame still needs clear line of sight and fuel. Pistol Fire dragging does not steer. Releasing or cancelling stops firing. Reload in the Stab position; Melee in Heavy. Desktop mouse aims, left click fires, right click reloads, Q returns to melee. Guard and Special are unavailable with the pistol.

Knife: Slash, Stab, Heavy, Pommel, Dodge and frontal Guard. Side and rear hits bypass defence.
